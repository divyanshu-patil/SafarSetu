Free tools (APIs):

•⁠ ⁠Maps: Leaflet or MapLibre with OpenStreetMap
•⁠ ⁠Place search: Nominatim or Photon
•⁠ ⁠Walking and road routes: OpenRouteService or OSRM
•⁠ ⁠Weather: Open-Meteo

Data files (datasets):

•⁠ ⁠PMPML buses: open data from the Pune Municipal Corporation, OpenCity, DataSpace or GitHub. Pick the newest one that matches the real app.
•⁠ ⁠Pune Metro: no API found, so type the timetable and fares into our own file from the official site.
•⁠ ⁠Local train: type the timetable in by hand for our demo route.
•⁠ ⁠Auto and cab fares: distance × official rate.
•⁠ ⁠Station tips and exits: our own research. explain me why we are choosing it
•⁠ ⁠for live traffic :Google Maps (Routes API) and TomTom
