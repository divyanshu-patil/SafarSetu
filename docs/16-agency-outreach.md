# 16 — Agency outreach kit

Turns "to approach" into action. **Nothing here has been sent.** Designations are standard official ones; confirm the current office-holder and contact page on each agency's own site before sending ([round-2 research §4](research/seva-research-round2.md)). No personal contacts are stored in this repo.

## 1. Where live data really stands (so we ask for the right thing)
- No public GTFS-Realtime feed or open live API was found for PMPML, BEST, Maha-Metro, MMRDA or Indian Railways. The PMPML and BEST timetables we use are **community GTFS** (MIT-0), built from the operators' apps; they are static.
- Therefore: the demo **simulates** delays and says so. For a pilot we need a **written data-sharing agreement**: a GTFS-Realtime (or equivalent) delay/cancellation feed plus permission to display it. This path is our synthesis, not a published process.

## 2. Data-sharing request (template)
> **To:** The Chairman & Managing Director, PMPML *(or: General Manager, BEST Undertaking)* — via the official contact/RTI-grievance page
> **Subject:** Request for a real-time delay feed to pilot a rider day-planner (Seva First Innovation Challenge, Maharashtra Zone)
>
> We are building **SafarSetu**, an open-timetable planner that helps commuters organise a day of work and errands around PMPML/BEST/metro/rail services and warns them calmly when a service is late or cancelled, in English, Hindi and Marathi. It uses the published community GTFS today; delays in our demo are simulated.
> We request: (1) a GTFS-Realtime TripUpdates/ServiceAlerts feed, or a daily export of delays and cancellations; (2) written permission to display it to riders with attribution; (3) a nodal officer for a **4-week, 30-user pilot** on the *[Hinjewadi–Shivajinagar]* corridor.
> We offer: free use of the planner and **anonymous, aggregated gap and reliability reports** for your service reviews; a data-protection notice aligned to the DPDP Act 2023; no resale of data.
> Attached: two-page note (problem, solution, method, how we will measure impact). Contact: *[name, email, phone]*.

## 2b. Pilot MoU — terms to propose (one page)
Scope (corridor, 4 weeks, 30 users) · data we receive and how it is shown/attributed · what we give back (monthly anonymised reliability + gap summary) · no personal data shared with the agency · data steward named on both sides · either party can stop with 7 days' notice · no payment in Stage 1; Stage 3 funded from the department's innovation allocation (below).

## 3. Why a department can say yes without new budget
- The **State Startup, Entrepreneurship & Innovation Policy 2025** asks each department to earmark **0.5 % of annual funds** for innovation, executed through MSInS; Startup Week pilot work orders are up to ₹25 lakh (secondary source, MED — verify on the policy text).
- SafarSetu **complements** existing spend (BEST ITMS ₹112 crore, FPJ 2019; PMPML tracking units ₹15,063.50 each via GeM, 2025): they own the hardware and feeds; we turn them into rider planning and gap evidence.
- **How a Seva work order is issued is undocumented** — ask the helpline in writing (support@sevainnovationchallenge.in) whether it is direct from MSInS or via GeM, and about DPIIT-recognition requirements for small vendors.
